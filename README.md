> Hiring a plumber, electrician or cleaner should not mean chasing quotes over phone calls and guessing who to trust. **Bidyard turns that mess into one clean flow:** post a job, receive bids, compare them side by side and hire with confidence.

<br>

## Why Bidyard

Most service marketplaces are built for one side and give the other side a basic form. Bidyard is designed around **both** people in the deal.

## Features

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

### For clients

**Post a job in minutes**
Four short steps: service, details, budget and date, then review. A live preview shows exactly what pros will see.

**Compare bids side by side**
Sort by lowest price, top rated or newest. Shortlist favorites and compare up to three bids, with the best price, rating and experience highlighted.

**Hire with confidence**
Open any provider's profile to see services, past projects and real reviews, then message them before you decide.

A clear progress tracker takes each job from posted to receiving bids, pro hired and completed.

Rate the work when the job is done and help the next client choose well.

Search and filter the feed by category, budget and whether parts are included. Save jobs to bid on later.

See which bids are active, won, lost or withdrawn, and where each client is in their decision.

## How it works

```
  Client posts a job  ──▶  Pros send bids  ──▶  Client compares and hires  ──▶  Work done, paid and reviewed
```

1. **Post.** The client describes the job, adds photos, picks a date and sets a budget.
2. **Bid.** Nearby pros see it in their feed and send a price, timeline and proposal.
3. **Compare.** The client sorts, shortlists, chats and compares bids side by side.
4. **Hire.** One tap accepts a bid and every other bidder is notified automatically.
5. **Complete.** Payment is released from escrow and both sides leave a review.

Both sides share the same live data. Post a job as a client, switch to the provider view and it is already waiting in the feed.

## Inside the app

<details>
<summary><b>Client screens</b></summary>
<br>

| Screen | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| My jobs | Dashboard with active jobs, bids received, completed jobs and total spend |
| Post a job | Four step form with a live preview |
| Job detail | Job info, progress tracker and every bid received |
| Compare bids | Up to three bids side by side with the best values highlighted |
| Provider profile | Services, portfolio, reviews and an invite button |

<details>
<summary><b>Provider screens</b></summary>
<br>

| Screen | What it does |
| --- | --- |

| Reviews | Rating summary and replies to client reviews |
